The heart behind Priyanka Beauty Parlour

Beauty with a Personal Touch

Priyanka Gupta brings over 15 years of beauty experience to Raxaul, including 8–10 years in bridal makeup. Her journey began with a cosmetics shop and grew through professional training in Patna.

Today, she combines personalised beauty services with boutique work and hands-on beautician, mehendi and stitching courses, helping women feel confident and learn new skills.

Each step has brought new ways to care, create and share.

  1. Where it began
